How the numbers work
Outlier
Views ÷ the channel's median views over its latest 30 uploads (Shorts compared with its Shorts; a channel that usually gets under 1,000 views counts as 1,000). 10× = ten times what that channel usually gets, so the subject pulled the views, not the channel's audience.
Heat
+48K · last 24 h = views gained since the previous day's reading (a count is stored with the time YouTube was read). +1.6M · since upload = all views of an upload under a day old. ~120K · avg/day (grey) = the average since upload, shown until a video has two daily readings; it overstates today's pace 2–5×, so the Heat sort leaves those out.
Climbing / cooling
Views gained in the last day against the video's own pace (its 7-day average, or the day before), compared with videos of the same age, since young uploads slow down by design. Climbing = 1.3× the usual for its age and 1,000+ views a day; cooling = 0.6× or less. The bars are the views gained on each of the last 14 days (today in light purple).
▲ / ▼
On the Remix score list: places moved in the list on screen since the previous day, once a video has two days of measured gains.
Breakouts
On Today: a fight with an upload from the last 7 days at 5× its channel's usual or more and 20K+ views. A row is one fight (uploads naming the same two fighters), with its best upload, this week's uploads and views, and how many breakdowns of it came out in the last 30 days.
Fresh from channel
An upload found in its channel's newest videos, already 5× that channel's usual, before it reached a top search list. Tracked = no longer in today's search results; its watch page was re-read so its line keeps going.
Proven
A remix (breakdown, story or reaction) about the same fighter or fight beat its channel's norm. Other creators: 20× = full marks. Your channel: 5× = full marks.
Remix score
100 × footage fit × (30% demand + 30% outlier + 25% heat + 15% proven). Demand = views on a log scale (10K → 0, 10M → 1). Outlier: 1× → 0, 100× → 1. Heat: 100/day → 0, 100K/day → 1. Bar colours: demand outlier heat proven.
Footage fit
UFC
Anything with UFC in the title, channel name or tags is removed. Videos that mention UFC only in the description are hidden unless switched on above. UFC name = a current UFC fighter or a past UFC title fighter is named in the title; check where the footage is from.
